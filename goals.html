Goal One

As a Writer

I want to build a bigger vocabulary and get the grammar rules right. My plan is to write pieces on my own, let other people read them, and learn from the feedback they give me. I also expect to learn just by doing this project and the ones that come after it.

1

Goal Two

At UCF

My goal is to make connections and meet people from all kinds of backgrounds, since UCF has a huge diversity of people. I plan to join clubs, both to meet people and to gain experience in my major. I also want to get into intramural sports, which will keep me active and social for my whole time here.

2

Goal Three

About Rhetoric

I want to be able to see how rhetors shape my actions and my thinking in places I am not familiar with, like online or out in public. I think knowing the rhetors around me will help me understand what a place actually means, and that skill should carry over into everyday life.
