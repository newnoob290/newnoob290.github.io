Goal One

What I want to accomplish by going to college

Answer in your own words. Name the degree or career you are aiming at, and the reason underneath it — not just "get a good job," but what you actually want to be able to do.

Goal Two

How writing helps me get there

Say honestly whether writing feels useful to your goals or not. If your field is technical, writing still shows up as reports, emails, proposals, and documentation — say where you expect to meet it.

Goal Three

What I want out of this course, beyond a grade

Name one or two real skills. For example: stop procrastinating on first drafts, learn to take feedback without taking it personally, or write faster without the quality dropping.

Goal Four

What I think this course will be like

What do you expect to be new here? What do you expect will repeat what high school already taught you? My guess is that the biggest change will be treating revision as the real work, not the cleanup.
