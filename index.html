I'm a first-year Electrical Engineering major at UCF, from Longwood, Florida. I'm here because I want to build things, and because I'd rather work on a hard problem than almost anything else.

Goal One

What I want to accomplish by going to college

I want my Electrical Engineering degree so I can invent things that help people. I like hard problems. I want to spend my life building things that didn't exist before, and that somebody is better off for having.

Goal Two

How writing helps me get there

Almost everything at work now happens in writing. Employers, coworkers, clients, it's all email and text. Nobody walks over to your desk anymore. So if I can't say what I mean in writing, it doesn't matter how good the idea is, because the written version is the only one anyone will ever see. For an engineer, writing is how the work gets delivered.

Goal Three

What I want out of this course, beyond a grade

I want to get an idea out of my head and have it land in somebody else's head sounding the way I meant it. There's a gap between what I mean and what people hear, and I want to close as much of it as I can.

Goal Four

What I think this course will be like

I think the new part will be studying how communication works, instead of just doing it. Why the same sentence lands differently on two different people, and how much of that I can actually control.

The review part is what I've been doing since middle school. Thesis statements, MLA citations, drafts and revisions.
