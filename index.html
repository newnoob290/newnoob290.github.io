Goal One

What I want to accomplish by going to college

I want my Electrical Engineering degree so I can invent things that help people. I've taught myself the software side already, mostly by building things and breaking them. What I can't teach myself is the math and theory underneath circuits and signals, and that's what I need if I want to design hardware instead of wiring parts together and hoping. The degree also gives me a reason for someone to trust me with real work, which I don't have yet. Right now I have projects and no credential. I want both.

1

Goal Two

How writing helps me get there

Almost everything at work now happens in writing. Employers, coworkers, clients, it's all email and text. Nobody walks over to your desk anymore. So if I can't say what I mean in writing, it doesn't matter how good the idea is, because the written version is the only one anyone will ever see. Engineering is full of this. Lab reports, specs, bug reports, the message you send when something breaks at midnight. All of it is writing, even when nobody calls it that.

2

Goal Three

What I want out of this course, beyond a grade

I want to get an idea out of my head and have it land in somebody else's head sounding the way I meant it. There's a gap between what I mean and what people hear, and I want to close as much of it as I can. I notice that gap most when I'm sure I was clear and the other person heard something else entirely. I don't expect it to disappear. I want to get better at predicting it, so I can write for the person reading instead of writing for myself and hoping.

3

Goal Four

What I think this course will be like

I think the new part will be studying how communication works instead of just doing it. Why the same sentence lands differently on two different people, and how much of that I can actually control. I've never taken a class that treated writing as something to examine rather than something to turn in. The review part is what I've been doing since middle school. Thesis statements, MLA citations, drafts and revisions. Professor Baker seems very passionate about teaching. She isn't here just to give us a grade, she's here to teach students life lessons.
