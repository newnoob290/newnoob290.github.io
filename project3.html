Major Project 3 · Due 7 December 2026

Reflecting on My Learning

The final assessment for this course. Below, each of the six course outcomes is paired with the specific piece of my own work that shows it.

Reflection

The six outcomes, one at a time

  • 1 · Writing processes and adaptation

    Point to one moment where you changed your process on purpose, and say why.

  • 2 · Multiple literacies and goal setting

    Name a literacy you already had — speaking, another language, video, music — and how you used it here.

  • 3 · Variation across contexts

    Compare how you write in two different places. A class essay and a group chat is a fine pair.

  • 4 · Decision making and production

    Describe one real choice you made for a specific audience, and the constraint that forced it.

  • 5 · Writing and power

    Where did language, identity, or authority shape what got heard in the community you studied?

  • 6 · Revision

    Quote one piece of feedback you received, say what you did with it, and say what you chose to ignore.

Looking Back

Where I started, where I ended

Return to the goals you set on the homepage. Did you meet them? Say plainly what changed and what did not.
